    10→## 1. Objective
    11→
    12→Create a **deterministic, repeatable benchmark** that measures what the
    13→EXISTING M4 memory/retrieval architecture (baseline **A0**) can and cannot
    14→retrieve, so the next architecture decision (PostgreSQL-native retrieval
    15→improvement, and only conditionally any graph layer) can be made from
    16→**measured evidence** instead of vendor claims or intuition.
    17→
    18→The benchmark answers, with numbers:
    19→
    20→1. What can M4 retrieve correctly today?
    21→2. Where does lexical retrieval fail?
    22→3. Where is semantic retrieval required?
    23→4. Where are temporal queries insufficient?
    24→5. Where do entity-centric queries fail?
    25→6. Where do multi-hop relationship queries fail?
    26→7. Where does provenance/authority fail?
    27→8. Where does Personal Mind vs Company Brain separation fail?
    28→9. What exact failures could justify a future associative/temporal graph layer?
    29→
    30→It does **not** assume Graphiti (or any graph) is the answer. It was built to
    31→make that question decidable later, on evidence.
    32→
    33→### Non-goals (enforced by construction)
    34→
    35→- No Graphiti, Neo4j, FalkorDB, or any graph database.
    36→- No pgvector / FTS / PostgreSQL retrieval implementation (baseline first).
    37→- No replacement or modification of the current memory system — **zero
    38→  `src/` files were changed** to build this harness.
    39→- No LLM output treated as authoritative company truth — no LLM is called
    40→  anywhere in the harness.

   195→## 6. Metrics
   196→
   197→Per query × surface (`metrics.ts`, pure functions pinned by hand-computed
   198→tests M1–M5):
   199→
   200→- **Recall@K** — K = the surface's production effective K (§2 table); facts
   201→  and claims lists are recorded in full so beyond-K misses remain visible and
   202→  classifiable.
   203→- **Precision@K** — gold fraction of the retrieved window (distractors count
   204→  against it; for the personal surface the tier-2 fallback fill is by design
   205→  and is annotated as such).
   206→- **MRR** — reciprocal rank of the first gold item per surface.
   207→- **Union recall** — gold items (any surface) present at rank ≤ K on their
   208→  surface: what a generation layer would actually have had to work with.
   209→- **Render recall** — gold items that actually rendered inside
   210→  `SophiaContextAssembler.assemble()` output (identity fingerprints per slice
   211→  authority): the e2e layer, including slice budgets and truncation.
   212→- **Correctness gates** (0/1, null when vacuous): authority-correctness,
   213→  temporal-correctness, supersession-correctness, founder-scope-correctness,
   214→  Personal-vs-Company boundary-correctness (§8–§9).
   215→
   216→Latency is NOT measured: the harness is a single deterministic process;
   217→wall-time would measure Bun startup, not retrieval. Deferred to a load-test
   218→harness (§14).
   219→
   220→## 7. Retrieval vs Generation Failure
   221→
   222→The harness contains **no model call**, so it structurally cannot produce
   223→GENERATION-class failures. The three-way distinction is enforced in the data
   224→model and every report:
   225→
   226→- **RETRIEVAL failure** — the correct evidence was never retrieved (or ranked
   227→  beyond the effective K): all 14 measured baseline failures are this class.
   228→- **AUTHORITY/LIFECYCLE failure** — unauthorized, superseded, or
   229→  lifecycle-ineligible evidence was retrieved or rendered in a truth-bearing
   230→  position, or scope/boundary leakage occurred: **zero measured** in A0.
   231→- **GENERATION failure** — correct evidence was in context but a model
   232→  produced a bad answer: **unmeasurable here by design**; self-test D6 asserts
   233→  the harness can never emit this class, and every report footer states it.
   234→  Measuring it requires a separate generation-side harness (§14).
   235→
   236→A key implication for reading the results: **recall/rank numbers here are the
   237→ceiling for answer quality** — a generation layer consuming this exact
   238→context cannot answer better than what was retrieved.
   239→
   240→## 8. Authority / Lifecycle Checks
   241→
   242→- **Authority-correctness** (hard gate, per M5 doc AUTH-1): the answer's
   243→  evidence must come from the authoritative surface; any personal-mind content
   244→  in a truth-bearing position, any pending claim as fact, or answering a
   245→  company question solely from non-authoritative material scores 0. Vacuous
   246→  (nothing retrieved) → null, not 0 — "honest failure" territory.
   247→- **Lifecycle enforcement** (measured, not assumed): PENDING_REVIEW /
   248→  SUPERSEDED / ARCHIVED / REJECTED personal memories must never enter the
   249→  ACTIVE pool or render; superseded facts must never appear in `listActiveFacts`
   250→  or render as current truth; predecessors must not be presented as current.
   251→- **Supersession-correctness**: for every declared pair
   252→  (FACT-OLD-01→FACT-CUR-01, PM-05→PM-06): successor present AND predecessor
   253→  absent from eligible truth positions.
   254→- **Founder-scope**: founder A's retrieval contains no founder-B records
   255→  (checked on every query), and a direct cross-founder `getMemory` probe must
   256→  fail closed (403).
   257→
   258→**Measured (A0): 10/10 authority, 13/13 founder-scope, 9/9 supersession,
   259→0 authority/lifecycle failures.** The M4 governance invariants hold under this
   260→workload — the failures are all retrieval-side.
   261→
   262→## 9. Personal vs Company Boundary Checks
   263→
   264→Structural, per query: personal evidence (PM-*) must never render under a
   265→truth-bearing authority (CANONICAL_FACT / AUTHORITATIVE_OPERATIONAL_STATE /
   266→COMPANY_KNOWLEDGE); company evidence must never render inside the
   267→`<personal_memory_context>` untrusted container. Advisory rendering of
   268→personal content (including the two deliberate traps PM-09/PM-11 inside the
   269→PERSONAL_MIND container) is by-design behavior, not a violation — the
   270→authority-only forbidden semantics encode exactly this.
   271→
   272→**Measured (A0): 13/13 boundary-correct.** Notably, the PM-09/PM-11 traps did
   273→render as advisory context for company queries (as the architecture intends) —
   274→the boundary held structurally in every case.
   275→
   276→## 10. Graph-Specific Failure Criteria
   277→
   278→A failure is flagged **graph-candidate** ONLY when ALL hold (deterministic
   279→rules, `classify.ts`; self-tests CL3/CL4 pin both polarities):
   280→
   281→1. the query declares a relationship hop at **depth ≥ 2** for the missed
   282→   evidence;
   283→2. **single-shot lexical overlap** between the query and the evidence text is
   284→   zero (no direct retrieval is possible even with perfect lexical scoring);
   285→3. the failure class is RETRIEVAL (not an authority violation).
   286→
   287→A failure is **never** tagged F merely because the query mentions
   288→relationships — the traversal requirement is checked against the declared
   289→chain and measured token overlap, not assumed. The fixture guarantees the
   290→criterion is testable: no record bridges both endpoints of the 2-hop chain.
   291→
   292→**Measured (A0): exactly 1 graph-candidate failure** — `FACT-DEP-01` (the
   293→Nimbus Gateway transitive dependency) in BQ3a. §12.5 analyzes whether it
   294→actually justifies a graph layer (short answer: not yet — a 2-hop lexical
   295→bridge exists through FACT-DEP-02, and the fact surface is not even
   296→query-conditioned yet).

   343→## 12. Failure Analysis
   344→
   345→### 12.1 Temporal (D ×2) — superseded truth is unreachable
   346→
   347→`listActiveFacts()` filters superseded facts and **no as-of / previous-version
   348→operator exists anywhere**. For "what was our previous pricing strategy",
   349→the old *policy documents and the precedent* happen to survive textually
   350→(knowledge/precedent surfaces), but the authoritative record of the previous
   351→price (FACT-OLD-01) is invisible to every surface. History questions are
   352→answerable only by luck of surviving prose, never from the governed chain —
   353→even though the chain (`supersededById` + promotedAt timestamps) fully
   354→exists in the data model.
   355→
   356→### 12.2 Change detection (B ×6, C ×2, D ×1 within BQ2's 9 failures) — no windowed change-set operator
   357→
   358→"What changed in the last 3 months" retrieves 4/13 gold: the three newest
   359→facts render only because the facts slice is unconditioned, and one knowledge
   360→doc matches the word "company". All five in-window precedents share **zero
   361→tokens** with the query; the decision record has **no render path at all**
   362→(CompanyState decisions are opaque JSON with no slice); CompanyState has no
   363→financial history to diff. There is no mechanism to enumerate
   364→promotions/supersessions/decisions in a time window.
   365→
   366→### 12.3 Episodic (J ×2) — no past-conversation retrieval surface
   367→
   368→The abandonment rationale exists verbatim in a past conversation
   369→(CONV-EP-01) with **lexical overlap 3** against BQ6a — and no code path can
   370→reach it: the canonical turn loads only the current conversation's last-10
   371→client-supplied turns. The precedent PREC-02 partially rescues the answer
   372→(50% recall) precisely because it duplicates the rationale in its outcome
   373→text. The anaphoric variant (BQ6b, "that approach") still retrieves PREC-02
   374→through the single surviving token "abandon" — lexical luck, not resolution.
   375→
   376→### 12.4 Structured filtering (C ×3) — unconditioned and opaque surfaces
   377→
   378→(a) The facts slice renders the **3 newest** facts regardless of the query —
   379→FACT-DEP-01 (rank 4 by recency) is unreachable for the Helix question even
   380→before traversal is considered. (b) CompanyState **decisions never render
   381→anywhere**. (c) Slice 1 renders metrics + top-3 active initiatives
   382→**unconditionally** — BQ8's "pass" is presence-by-construction, not query
   383→understanding.
   384→
   385→### 12.5 The single graph-candidate failure — honest scope
   386→
   387→FACT-DEP-01 (Nimbus Gateway → Aurorium Auth → Helix Identity Store, hop
   388→depth 2) failed with zero single-shot lexical overlap. **This does NOT yet
   389→justify a graph layer**, for three measured reasons:
   390→
   391→1. The intermediate hop (FACT-DEP-02) shares "Aurorium Auth Service" tokens
   392→   with both endpoints — a **2-step lexical expansion** (retrieve DEP-02,
   393→   re-query with its terms) would reach it; no persistent edge store is
   394→   required to close this gap.
   395→2. The fact surface is not even query-conditioned (C) — until facts can be
   396→   selected by relevance, traversal quality is unmeasurable.
   397→3. The edges themselves are **prose** ("depends on …") inside fact
   398→   statements — an explicit `dependency` relation over existing rows (a
   399→   relational column or join table) represents this chain without any graph
   400→   database, consistent with the M5 evaluation's derived-index rule.
   401→
   402→Graphiti-class tooling becomes a candidate only if, after the §13 experiment,
   403→the multi-hop class still fails AND dependency/entity queries become
   404→product-critical for the founder.
   405→
   406→### 12.6 Ranking inversions (measured, no hard failure)
   407→
   408→The retired v1 pricing doc outranks the current v2 for both "right now"
   409→(BQ4) and "currently true" (BQ5a) questions — knowledge MRR 0.50 both times.
   410→Lexical overlap cannot express temporal currency. Similarly BQ7b's type-scoped
   411→question falls to the unconditioned fallback because the M4-D fold
   412→deliberately never matches `preferences`↔`prefers` (homograph protection).
   413→
   414→### 12.7 Precision observations
   415→
   416→Personal-surface precision is 13–75% (tier-2 fallback fills by design —
   417→contextual noise, not a correctness bug); BQ3a's knowledge window is 100%
   418→distractor (the Helix data contract matched "helix"). These quantify the
   419→context pollution a generation layer must resist.
   420→
   421→### 12.8 Scale sensitivity — what this benchmark can NOT yet claim
   422→
   423→The corpus is deliberately small. Three "passes" are scale-limited:
   424→
   425→- **BQ4 (entity-centric) 100%**: only 6 knowledge docs and 6 precedents
   426→  exist, FACT-CUR-01 happens to be the 2nd-newest fact, and the initiative
   427→  renders unconditionally. Adding a handful of Lumora-mentioning docs would
   428→  push gold out of the limit-2 windows.
   429→- **BQ7 (personal) 100%**: 8 active memories all fit the 20-cap and the
   430→  1200-char render budget, so tier-2 fallback masks paraphrase misses for
   431→  RECALL — the semantic weakness shows only in RANK (PM-10 at 8/8 for
   432→  BQ7a). The starvation regime (>20 active) is pinned by the existing m4c
   433→  C4/H9 cap tests, not by this fixture.
   434→- **Render ≡ union recall**: at this scale nothing retrieved was lost in
   435→  render; H_context_assembly did not manifest.
   436→
   437→A scale-expanded fixture (100+ memories/docs) is deferred work (§14); the
   438→mechanism-level numbers above are the valid claims of THIS run.
   439→
