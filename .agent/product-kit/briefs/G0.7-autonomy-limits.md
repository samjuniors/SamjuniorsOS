## Kill switch (proposal)
Saying "stop" by voice, or pressing the panel button, immediately freezes new autonomous actions, cancels in-flight tool calls where possible, records the state, and keeps everything read-only until you say resume. It must work with the model provider down, so it is deterministic code. Each gate that adds autonomy drills it.

## Approval design
Risk-tiered, with batched digests, so approvals stay meaningful (Inference: too many prompts lead to rubber-stamping). Approvals expire and are bound to the exact payload hash, which the repository already does.

## Evidence
- **Verified:** the repo has payload-bound approvals, idempotency records and a side-effect audit; I found no pause or kill control and no hash-chained audit.
- **Reported:** the OWASP Agent Control Standard (v0.1 preview, Sept 2026) describes runtime enforcement hooks and a policy authority. Small open-source projects (Latch, AgentPolicy) return allow, block or approve and keep rolling per-agent budgets. Paperclip documents budget hard-stops with pause and terminate.

## The attack
- Too strict and nothing is autonomous: you become the bottleneck. Too loose and one bad run costs money or reputation. Graduation by evidence (G15.8) is the way between, and it needs the evaluation harness first.
- A $0 financial limit means Sofia cannot run even a small paid experiment. Consider a small experiment cap once the kill switch drill passes.
- Whether customers must be told they are dealing with an AI varies by place. Unknown; a qualified person must review it (G15.6).
