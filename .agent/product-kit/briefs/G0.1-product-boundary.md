# G0.1 brief: internal-only or also a public product?

Status: DECIDED 2026-10-20: internal for now, designed so more founders can be added later, nothing multi-founder implemented (recorded in `done/G0.1.json`, with `ack/NA-G10.json`). The analysis below is kept as the record. Nothing here changes `PRODUCT.md`. Confidence labels: **Verified** (read in the repo or a primary source this session), **Reported** (secondary source), **Inference**, **Unknown**.

## Decision needed
`PRODUCT.md` section 1 locks SamJuniorsOS as internal and single-founder. Commercial SaaS, multi-tenancy, public signup and billing are explicit non-goals in `doc/ROADMAP.md`. You have said you want a product that real users can use. That is a change of boundary and needs your decision and an ADR.

## What is true today
- **Verified:** the governance core is real and tested: approval bound to a payload hash, a deterministic verifier, a decision store, an audit ledger, a bounded council. The roadmap's own FOUNDATION table says what is implemented.
- **Verified:** the problem statement in `PRODUCT.md` is your own. No other user is named anywhere. Demand from other founders is **Unknown**.
- **Verified:** the system is not yet deployable for others: SQLite plus file stores, single instance, no multi-tenancy, and persistence on a hosted target is undecided.

## Evidence from outside
- **Reported:** "AI employee" products for solo founders already sell at roughly $50 to $200 a month (Lindy about $50 to $200; Sintra's bundle $97) [layer3labs.io/guides/ai-employee-platforms-compared, updated Jul 20, 2026]. Comparison sites like this are often affiliate-driven; prices change.
- **Reported, low confidence:** one competitor-run review says Sintra's "employees" are personas rather than autonomous agents [autonomous.ai/en-CA/ourblog/sintra-ai-review]. The source sells a competing product.
- **Verified by multiple reprints of the release (I did not open gartner.com itself):** Gartner forecast in June 2025 that over 40% of agentic AI projects will be canceled by the end of 2027, citing escalating costs, unclear business value and inadequate risk controls, and estimated only about 130 of thousands of agentic vendors are real. A forecast, about enterprises.
- **Verified:** OWASP published a Top 10 for Agentic Applications on 2025-12-09 [genai.owasp.org/resource/owasp-top-10-for-agentic-applications-for-2026].
- **Reported:** Windows 11 now ships an opt-in "Hey Copilot" wake word, Copilot Vision, and Copilot Actions, with agents running under a separate account [thurrott.com/a-i/328424/...]. Voice plus screen plus PC actions is becoming an operating-system feature.

## The attack
1. **N = 1.** The only evidence of the problem is your own use. Nothing yet shows other founders want governed agents enough to pay for them. This is the central unknown.
2. **The category is crowded and cheap.** A buyer can already get "AI employees" for about the price of a streaming bundle. A governance-first product must be worth a visible premium or serve a buyer those products do not.
3. **Your real differentiator is governance, not voice.** Deterministic approvals, payload-bound consent and an audit ledger address what the Gartner forecast names (risk controls). That is a hypothesis for solo founders, not a finding. The voice and PC-control layer is the part the platform vendors are bundling.
4. **A public product costs about as much again as everything built so far:** multi-tenancy, multi-user auth, billing, legal documents, support, abuse handling. You are one person using AI agents to build.
5. **Two products at once can fail both.** The internal OS does not yet run your company end to end. Splitting attention before it does risks neither working.

## Options
- **A. Internal only for now.** Finish G1 to G5, run your company on it for a fixed period, then decide with real usage data.
- **B. Internal plus design partners.** After the core loop works, offer it to three to five other solo founders as separate single-tenant installs. No multi-tenant build. Tests the one hypothesis that matters (point 3).
- **C. Public product now.** Highest cost, highest risk, no demand evidence.

## Recommended default (Inference)
A, then B. Keep the boundary locked until the Founder Operating Loop (G5) is in daily use by you with measured outcomes. Then run G10.1 (validation with real users) before any multi-tenant work.

## What would change this recommendation
- Concrete evidence that other founders will pay for governed agents, from conversations you have held.
- A narrow customer type you can name in one sentence.
- Platform bundling making the internal tool redundant for your own needs (then reconsider the whole project).

## Questions for you
1. How many other founders have asked for this, and what exactly did they ask?
2. How long should the internal trial run before you decide, and what result counts as success?
3. Is the goal revenue, or a tool you trust to run your company? The two lead to different roadmaps.
