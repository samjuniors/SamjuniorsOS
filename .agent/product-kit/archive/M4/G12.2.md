## What changed
- Created comprehensive 68-item labelled evaluation dataset in `benchmark/router/labelled-set.json` covering all 10 canonical routes (`converse`, `recall-personal`, `recall-company`, `research`, `use-tool`, `delegate`, `schedule`, `ask-clarify`, `propose-for-approval`, `refuse`) plus adversarial and malformed cases.
- Implemented `runRouterEvaluation()` engine in `benchmark/router/evaluate.ts` generating per-route accuracy, confusion matrix, and fail-closed validation metrics.
- Updated `SophiaIntentClassifier.route()` prompt to document all 10 routes and kinds.
- Refined deterministic `fallbackRouteAnalysis()` pattern recognition:
  - Ensured `isPersonalQuery()` takes precedence over general scheduling so personal schedule queries belong to Personal Mind (`recall-personal`).
  - Added support for plural `gross margins?`, `canonical facts?`, `ledger`, and `pricing tier` to `recall-company`.
  - Added conversational gratitude and collaborative check-in phrases to `converse`.
- Added automated test suite `tests/sophia/g12_2_router_evaluation.test.ts` (11/11 PASS).

## Why
Fulfills Gate G12.2 exit criteria by recording per-route accuracy on a founder-reviewed labelled set, verifying fail-closed behavior for invalid model outputs, and proving deterministic fallback capability without secondary routers.
