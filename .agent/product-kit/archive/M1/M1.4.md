## What changed
1. `src/lib/server/sophia/voice-latency.ts`:
   - Created `VoiceLatencyTracker` singleton that stores voice and typing turn metrics, persists to the `'voice_latency'` collection in `DurableFileStore`, and computes summary statistics (sampleCount, p50, p95, p99, min, max, mean).
   - Implemented standard linear interpolation `calculatePercentile` utility function for deterministic p50 and p95 computation.
2. `src/lib/server/sophia/index.ts`:
   - Exported `voice-latency` module.
3. `src/lib/server/live/types.ts`:
   - Added `turnStartTime` and `turnStopTime` to `LiveSessionRecord`.
4. `src/lib/server/live/server.ts`:
   - Wired `START_PTT` and `STOP_PTT` to capture session timestamps.
   - Wired `handleFinalTranscript` to record server-side STT, cognition, and gateway turnaround latencies to `VoiceLatencyTracker` and forward them in `SOPHIA_RESPONSE.metrics`.
5. `src/sofia/sophia/types.ts`:
   - Added `endToEndMs`, `endToEndFromStopMs`, `turnId`, and `record` to `SophiaEventDetail`.
6. `src/sofia/sophia/voice/LiveGatewayVoiceProvider.ts`:
   - Added phase timing hooks for PTT press, PTT release, final transcript, response arrival, TTS synthesis, and audio playback start.
   - Computed conversational end-to-end latency (from PTT release / speech finish to playback start) and total turn latency.
   - Recorded metrics in `latencyRecords` and emitted them with `audio_started`.
   - Added `getLatencySummary()`, `getLatencyRecords()`, and `clearLatencyRecords()` helper methods.
7. `src/app/api/sofia/voice/latency/route.ts` & `src/app/api/sophia/voice/latency/route.ts`:
   - Added authenticated GET and POST endpoints for latency telemetry.
8. `tests/sophia/m1_4_voice_loop.test.ts`:
   - Authored complete test suite covering:
     - Group 1: PTT voice loop from speech to AudioEngine playback on Windows PC.
     - Group 2: Latency measurement over 25 turns reporting p50 and p95 with mathematical validation.
     - Group 3: Typing coexistence via `sendText` and alternating voice/typing sequences.
     - Group 4: Authenticated latency API endpoints.
     - Group 5: Preserved model fallbacks (Gemini, OpenAI, LM Studio, Fake).

## Why
Delivers the core voice loop on the Windows PC with push-to-talk, streaming STT, and spoken replies, and establishes an empirical latency baseline (p50 and p95 over >= 20 turns) as required by G6.2 and M1.4 while deferring barge-in, turn detection, and latency optimization.
