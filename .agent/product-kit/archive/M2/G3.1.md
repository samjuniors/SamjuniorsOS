## What changed
- `src/lib/server/authorization/gate.ts`:
  - Exported canonical `ALLOWED_FOUNDER_IDENTITIES` and `isAuthorizedFounderIdentity` validator.
  - Eliminated loose `(actor.startsWith('founder-') && !actor.includes('agent'))` prefix match in `decideApproval` and `resolveInterruptedOperation`.
  - Replaced the role blocklist (`prohibitedRoles`) in `revokeApproval` with strict allowlist enforcement.
  - Added optional `userContext` to `RevokeApprovalParams`.
- `src/app/api/workflow/approvals/route.ts`:
  - Forwarded verified session `userContext` to `gate.revokeApproval`.
- `.env.example`:
  - Documented live Clerk authentication provisioning variables (`NEXT_PUBLIC_CLERK_PUBLISHABLE_KEY` and `CLERK_SECRET_KEY`).
- `src/proxy.ts`:
  - Expanded `EXECUTIVE_API_PATTERNS` to cover `/api/conversation`, `/api/sofia/memory`, `/api/sophia/system/action`, and `/api/auth/ws-ticket`.
  - Refined `hasProvider` check to require complete Clerk keys (`CLERK_SECRET_KEY` and `NEXT_PUBLIC_CLERK_PUBLISHABLE_KEY`) or `SAMJUNIORS_DEV_SECRET`.
  - Strictly fails closed (401) on unauthenticated or role-override requests in production.
- `tests/sophia/g3_1_auth_gaps.test.ts`:
  - Added 16 automated tests covering prefix spoofing rejection, blocklist elimination, allowlisted founder admittance, live Clerk documentation, proxy fail-closed matrix, and route-level direct invocation fail-closed semantics.

## Why
- Gap 1: Previously, any string starting with `founder-` (e.g. `founder-attacker`) bypassed authorization checks in `decideApproval` and `resolveInterruptedOperation`, and any unlisted role (e.g. `attacker`, `admin`) could revoke approvals due to a blocklist rather than an allowlist.
- Gap 2: Live production deployments require complete identity provider credentials or server dev secrets; previously `.env.example` lacked `CLERK_SECRET_KEY`, and certain sensitive executive endpoints were missing from the edge proxy's executive patterns.
