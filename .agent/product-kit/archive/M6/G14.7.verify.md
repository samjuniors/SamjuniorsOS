## Detailed Findings

### 1. Contract Tests (Schemas & Envelopes)
- `src/types/budget.ts`:
  - `RunawayBudgetConfigSchema` deterministically validates positive integer constraints for `maxCallsPerRun` (default 25), `maxCostCentsPerRun` (default 500¢), `maxCallsPerMinute` (default 30), and `maxConsecutiveFailures` (default 5). Negative values are strictly rejected.
  - `ToolOutputEnvelopeSchema` strictly enforces provenance with `isUntrusted: z.literal(true)`, `sourceCapabilityId`, `sanitized`, `injectionMarkersDetected`, and `timestamp`. Untrusted provenance cannot be spoofed to false.
  - Specialized error classes `RunawayToolLoopError` and `CapabilityBudgetExceededError` provide rich forensic metadata (run ID, capability ID, call count, threshold, cap cents).
- `src/types/capability.ts`:
  - `CapabilityInvocationRequestSchema` adds optional `runId` and `budgetConfig`.
  - `CapabilityInvocationResultSchema` extends `status` with `'budget_exceeded'` and includes optional `envelope: ToolOutputEnvelopeSchema`.
- Contract tests in `tests/capabilities/g14_7_capability_evaluation.test.ts` (Section 1) verify schema validation, negative bound rejection, and envelope wrapping.

### 2. Indirect Prompt Injection Defense
- `src/lib/server/capabilities/tool-output-sanitizer.ts`:
  - `ToolOutputSanitizer` recursively inspects arbitrary output (strings, arrays, nested objects) against 10 injection regex patterns:
    - `SYSTEM_OVERRIDE` (`/\[SYSTEM(?:\s+OVERRIDE)?\]/i`)
    - `SYSTEM_HEADER` (`/(?:^|\n)\s*SYSTEM:\s*/i`)
    - `INSTRUCTION_TAG` (`/\[(?:INSTRUCTION|INST)\]|<\/?instruction>/i`)
    - `ADMIN_OVERRIDE` (`/ADMIN(?:\s+OVERRIDE|\s+COMMAND)/i`)
    - `IGNORE_PREVIOUS` (`/IGNORE\s+(?:ALL\s+)?(?:PREVIOUS|PRIOR)\s+INSTRUCTIONS/i`)
    - `DISREGARD_RULES` (`/DISREGARD\s+(?:ALL\s+)?(?:RULES|CONSTRAINTS|POLICIES)/i`)
    - `FORCED_TOOL_INVOCATION` (`/(?:invoke|call|execute)\s+tool:[a-z0-9_:-]+/i`)
    - `FORCED_APPROVAL` (`/(?:approve|bypass)\s+(?:all\s+)?pending\s+approvals?/i`)
    - `XML_SYSTEM_TAG` (`/<\/?(?:system|developer|admin)>/i`)
    - `SYSTEM_CODEBLOCK` (`/```(?:system|prompt|instruction)/i`)
  - Detected markers are flagged in `envelope.injectionMarkersDetected` and sanitized via `neutralizeDelimiters` / `sanitizePayload` while preserving underlying business data.
  - `formatForPrompt()` guarantees strict lexical framing with `<<<UNTRUSTED_EXTERNAL_TOOL_OUTPUT>>>` boundaries.
- **Architectural Immunity**:
  - Crucially, tool outputs have zero execution authority. Even if an LLM is exposed to adversarial text commanding it to perform side effects:
    1. Financial side effects (`tool:stripe:create_payment_intent` / `financial_legal`) cannot execute; they are halted by `CapabilityGateway` in `awaiting_founder_approval` with a cryptographic `payloadHash`.
    2. External communications (`tool:resend:send_email` / `external_hard_to_reverse`) cannot execute; they are halted in `awaiting_founder_approval`.
    3. Destructive actions (`destructive_security`) are denied under Autonomy L0 policy or held in approval.
    4. Client or LLM claims cannot forge Founder approvals.

### 3. Runaway Tool Loop & Cost Caps
- `src/lib/server/capabilities/budget-guard.ts`:
  - `CapabilityBudgetGuard` tracks run state across 4 orthogonal dimensions:
    1. `callCount` vs `maxCallsPerRun`: Halts runaway loops when call count reaches the limit.
    2. `cumulativeCostCents` vs `maxCostCentsPerRun`: Halts pre-invocation if next call would exceed the remaining financial budget.
    3. `callTimestamps` vs `maxCallsPerMinute`: 60-second sliding window rate limiting prevents high-frequency burst loops.
    4. `consecutiveFailures` vs `maxConsecutiveFailures`: Detects broken retry loops without recovery. Resets failure counter to 0 on success.
- `src/lib/server/capabilities/capability-gateway.ts`:
  - Pre-invocation check halts budget violations with `status: 'budget_exceeded'`.
  - Violations are durably recorded to `InMemoryAuditStore` with `decision: 'denied'` and reason codes `DENIED_BUDGET_CAP_EXCEEDED` or `DENIED_RUNAWAY_LOOP_DETECTED`.
  - Post-invocation updates run statistics for both success and failure paths.

### 4. CI Suite Integration

  - Because `tests/capabilities/g14_7_capability_evaluation.test.ts` lives under `tests/capabilities/` and contains `.test.`, it is automatically part of the canonical CI test suite.

### 5. Quality Bar & Smallest-Change Ladder

- Clean backward compatibility: existing capability invocations without `runId` or `budgetConfig` default to safe company bounds (25 calls, $5.00 limit).
