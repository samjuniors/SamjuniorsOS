## What changed
1. `src/os/lib/surfaceSchema.ts`:
   - Added typed `ApprovalData` and `CockpitData` contracts.
2. `src/os/lib/osStore.ts`:
   - Added ephemeral `SyncStatus` ("idle" | "loading" | "error" | "synced") and sync error states.
   - Partitioned `cachedDecisions` (`!d.approvalId`) and `cachedApprovals` (`!!d.approvalId`).
   - Exported `openApprovals`, `syncStatusState`, `activitySyncStatusState`, and actions `setSyncStatus`, `setActivitySyncStatus`.
3. `src/os/lib/runtime.ts`:
   - `syncFromServer()` sets `syncStatus` and `activitySyncStatus` to `"loading"` on start, `"synced"` on success, and `"error"` on failure.
4. `src/os/components/surfaces/StandardSurfaces.tsx`:
   - Exported `ApprovalSurface`: displays amber seal ("Waiting on Founder"), requesting role, payload binding preview (SHA-256 bound hash), explicit voice refusal advisory notice, and Approve/Reject buttons wired to server decisions.
   - Exported `CockpitSurface`: combines spend counter vs daily cap ($X / $Y cap), heartbeat fresh status, attention deck, and active workstreams, with Loading, Empty, and Error states.
5. `src/os/components/FlowDesktop.tsx`:
   - `AttentionList`: renders `LoadingState` ("Synchronizing company operational state..."), `ErrorState` ("Telemetry Stream Disrupted" + retry), and `EmptyState` ("All operations nominal").
   - `DecisionList`: renders `LoadingState` ("Retrieving open decision briefs..."), `ErrorState` ("Governance Queue Inaccessible" + retry), and `EmptyState` ("No open decisions").
   - `ApprovalList`: renders dedicated side-effect approvals card with `LoadingState` ("Verifying pending cryptographic authorization gates..."), `ErrorState` ("Authorization Gate Unreachable" + retry), and `EmptyState` ("No pending approvals").
   - `CompanyActivityList`: renders `LoadingState` ("Streaming immutable audit ledger..."), `ErrorState` ("Audit Stream Unavailable" + retry), and `EmptyState` ("No activity recorded").
6. `tests/frontend/fol_surface_states.test.ts`:
   - 16/16 tests verifying all operational states, payload hash previews, voice refusal notice, and store state partitioning.

## Why
Per `AGENTS.md` and `docs/FOL_V1_ACCEPTANCE_JOURNEYS.md`, the Founder Operating Loop v1 requires clean, honest, server-authoritative interfaces across Cockpit, Decisions, Approvals, and Activity, strictly adhering to fail-closed security and 0% voice approval on consequential side-effects.
