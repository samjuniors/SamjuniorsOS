## Smallest-change ladder
Needed: Invariant that consequential actions can only be approved through authenticated visual panel or verified phone sign-off; voice channels can propose but can never approve.
Already in codebase: `SideEffectAuthorizationGate`, `InMemoryApprovalStore`, `PostgresApprovalStore`, `SophiaServerGateway`, `executeSophiaTurn`, `LiveInteractionServer`.
Platform or dependency: TypeScript, Next.js, Node.js.
Not built: Complex biometric voice signing; per `AGENTS.md` §4, §6, §11, voice is treated as an untrusted acoustic interface, and approval requires out-of-band visual verification.
Added: Channel attribution on approval records, fail-closed guards in `SideEffectAuthorizationGate` and approval stores rejecting `channel: 'voice'`, immediate voice approval refusal in `SophiaServerGateway` and `/api/realtime/turn`, and attribution propagation through `turn-executor` and `LiveInteractionServer`.

## What changed
1. Extended `FounderApprovalRecord` and gate types in `src/types/authorization.ts` and `src/lib/server/authorization/gate.ts`:
   - Added `channel?: 'panel' | 'phone' | 'voice' | 'api' | string` to record metadata.
   - Enforced fail-closed error in `SideEffectAuthorizationGate.decideApproval`: `Voice approval rejected: Consequential actions cannot be approved by voice (Gate G6.4). Approvals must be decided through panel or phone.`
2. Hardened `PostgresApprovalStore` and `InMemoryApprovalStore` in `src/lib/server/authorization/approval-store.ts`:
   - Updated `decide()` signature to accept `channel?: 'panel' | 'phone' | 'voice' | 'api' | string`.
   - Enforced fail-closed rejection whenever `channel === 'voice' && decision === 'approved'`, leaving the record strictly in `pending` status.
   - Preserved `channel` on updated approval records for full audit trail integrity.
3. Updated `SophiaServerGateway` in `src/lib/server/sophia/server-gateway.ts`:
   - Added `channel` and `ingress` options to `GatewayProcessOptions`.
   - When handling `approval_proposal` where `decision === 'approved'` and `channel === 'voice'` or `ingress === 'live_voice' | 'voice'`, immediately refuses approval without touching `approvalStore` or disambiguating candidates.
   - Returns clear refusal: `Voice approval refused: Consequential actions cannot be approved by voice. Please sign off on the Approvals panel or via verified phone sign-off.`
4. Threaded `channel` through `executeSophiaTurn` and `LiveInteractionServer`:
   - In `src/lib/server/sophia/turn-executor.ts`: Added `channel?: 'panel' | 'phone' | 'voice' | 'api'` to `ExecuteSophiaTurnOptions`. Derived `effectiveChannel = opts.channel || (opts.ingress === 'live_voice' || opts.ingress === 'voice' ? 'voice' : undefined)` and forwarded to gateway.
   - In `src/lib/server/live/server.ts`: Explicitly passed `ingress: 'live_voice'` and `channel: 'voice'` on `turnExecutor()` calls.
   - In `src/app/api/workflow/approvals/route.ts`: Defaulted `channel: body.channel || 'panel'`.
5. Authored comprehensive test suite `tests/sophia/g6_4_voice_approval_refusal.test.ts` (10/10 PASS):
   - Proved spoken approval via `executeSophiaTurn(ingress: 'live_voice')` refuses approval and leaves approval record pending.
   - Proved spoken approval via `executeSophiaTurn(channel: 'voice')` refuses approval and leaves approval record pending.
   - Proved spoken approval via `LiveInteractionServer.executeTurn()` refuses approval and leaves approval record pending.
   - Proved voice turn via HTTP `POST /api/realtime/turn` returns `voice_approval_refused` and leaves approval record pending.
   - Proved direct call to `SideEffectAuthorizationGate.decideApproval(channel: 'voice')` throws fail-closed exception.
   - Proved direct call to `InMemoryApprovalStore.decide(channel: 'voice')` throws fail-closed exception.
   - Proved voice can PROPOSE actions that require approval (directive created in prepare_only / proposal mode, without satisfying approval gate).
   - Proved authenticated desktop panel sign-off (`channel: 'panel'`) satisfies approval gate.
   - Proved authenticated phone sign-off (`channel: 'phone'`) satisfies approval gate.
   - Proved speech prompt injection attempting to claim panel or phone identity in utterance tokens fails closed.
