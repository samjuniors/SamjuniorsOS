## 1. Summary of Deliverables

1. **Route-Aware Scope & Partition Enforcement (`SophiaContextAssembler.assemble`)**:
   - Extended `SophiaContextAssembler.assemble` options with `route?: SophiaRoute` and `budgetLimit?: number` (`src/lib/server/sophia/context-assembly.ts`).
   - For all company routes (`recall-company`, `research`, `use-tool`, `delegate`, `schedule`, `propose-for-approval`): `includePersonal` is strictly `false`. Personal Mind is never queried from `SophiaMemoryStore`, no personal slices are emitted, and personal canaries never leak into company answers even when `founderId` is supplied.
   - For `recall-personal`: `includeCompany` is strictly `false`. Company Brain slices (operational state, active workflows, company knowledge) are completely excluded.
   - For `converse`, `ask-clarify`, and `refuse`: zero memory slices are assembled (`scopes: []`, `sources: []`).
   - Route-based enforcement strictly overrides permissive defaults (`scope: "all"`).

2. **Route-Specific Budget Allocation (`getPartitionLimitsForRoute`)**:
   - Exported `DEFAULT_PARTITION_LIMITS` and implemented `getPartitionLimitsForRoute(route?: SophiaRoute, budgetLimitTokens?: number)`.
   - Zero-allocates forbidden partitions per route (e.g. `personalMind: 0` for all company routes; company partitions = 0 for personal/converse/refuse/ask-clarify).
   - Tailors budget ceilings to route domains:
     - `research`: expanded `companyKnowledge` (2400 chars) and `canonicalFacts` (1600 chars).
     - `delegate` & `schedule`: prioritized `activeWorkflows` (1600-1800 chars).
     - `propose-for-approval`: prioritized `pendingGovernance` (2000 chars) and `operationalState` (1400 chars).
     - `recall-personal`: prioritized `personalMind` (2000 chars).
   - Dynamic proportional scaling ensures custom `budgetLimit` is respected without starvation.

3. **Scopes and Sources Reporting on Every Answer (`SophiaTurnExecutor`)**:
   - `executeSophiaTurn` passes `route` and `budgetLimit` to `SophiaContextAssembler.assemble`.
   - Answers reading scopes include `[Scopes: ... | Sources: ...]` chip.
   - Every answer returns typed `scopes` (`MemoryScope[]`), `sources` (`string[]`), `sourceIds` (`string[]`), and attached `decisionLog`.
   - Durable message persistence in `ConversationStore` records `metadata.scopes`, `metadata.sources`, `metadata.sourceIds`, and `metadata.decisionLog`.

4. **Automated Verification Test Suite**:
   - Authored `tests/sophia/g12_3_context_assembly_per_route.test.ts` (19/19 tests PASS).
