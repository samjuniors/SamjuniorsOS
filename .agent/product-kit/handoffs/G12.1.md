## What changed
- Codified `SOPHIA_ROUTES` with 10 canonical routes (`converse`, `recall-personal`, `recall-company`, `research`, `use-tool`, `delegate`, `schedule`, `ask-clarify`, `propose-for-approval`, `refuse`).
- Codified `RouteMemoryScope` (`personal`, `company`, `role_brain`, `none`) enforcing that `personal` scope is strictly restricted to `recall-personal`.
- Codified `RouteSideEffectClass` (`read_only`, `internal_write`, `external_reversible`, `external_hard_to_reverse`, `financial_legal`, `destructive_security`).
- Codified `DECISION_ROUTE_TAXONOMY` with entry conditions, scopes, side effects, and human-in-the-loop requirements for every route.
- Extended `SophiaIntentClassifier.route()` to recognize all 10 routes, failing closed to `ask-clarify` for unknown routes or malformed payloads.
- Added deterministic fallback classification in `fallbackRouteAnalysis()` for all extended routes.

## Why
Ensures that Sofia routes queries to explicitly governed execution lanes with strict memory scope isolation, fail-closed security for adversarial or invalid inputs, and a single authoritative classifier architecture.
