6. `tests/quality/fol_vertical_slice.test.ts`:
   - Authored complete 15-test integration suite covering Journeys J1–J5:
     - **J1 (Cockpit Ingress):** Health latency <= 1500ms, scheduler status, authentic attention items, model spend ceiling.
     - **J2 (Council Directive):** Intent classified as `directive`, Executive Council deliberation (Thorne, Maya, Julian, Sophia COO), `ConstitutionalVerifier` validates margin floor (85% >= 80%) and deliverable provenance.

     - **J4 (Strategic Decisions):** Decision registered, resolved with outcome via `/api/decisions/:id/resolve`, durably persisted.
     - **J5 (Epistemic Truth & Crash Recovery):** In-flight approval records survive simulated reboot without data loss; activity ledger projection maintains zero sequence or anchor drift.
