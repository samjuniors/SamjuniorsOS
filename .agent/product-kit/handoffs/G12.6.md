## What changed
1. `prisma/schema.prisma`: Added `model DecisionLogEntry` with table mapping `@@map("decision_logs")` containing `id`, `turnId`, `messageId`, `conversationId`, `actor`, `query`, `route`, `confidence`, `divertedFromRoute`, `isLowConfidenceClarification`, `policyResult`, `outcome`, `scopes`, `sources`, `reasoning`, `executionRef`, `replayPayload`, `createdAt`, `updatedAt`, and indexes on `[conversationId, createdAt]`, `[route, createdAt]`, `[outcome]`, and `[turnId]`.
2. `src/types/decision.ts`: Added `DecisionPolicyEvaluation`, `DecisionPolicyResult`, and `DecisionLogOutcomeKind`.
3. `src/lib/server/sophia/decision-log.ts`: Updated `SophiaDecisionLogEntry`, `buildDecisionLogEntry`, `formatDecisionLogExplanation`, `formatNoDecisionLogExplanation`, and `extractExplanationQueryLogId`.
4. `src/lib/server/sophia/decision-log-store.ts`: Authored new authoritative singleton store implementing `recordDecision`, `getDecision`, `getDecisionByTurnId`, `getLatestDecision`, `queryDecisions`, `replayDecision`, and `clearForTest` with Prisma persistence, local `DurableFileStore` durability, and in-memory cache.
5. `src/lib/server/sophia/turn-executor.ts`: Enforced "no log entry means no action" fail-closed invariant; records every turn and decision to `DecisionLogStore`; explanation queries cite authoritative decision log entries; fail-closed try/catch halts execution if decision log persistence fails.
6. `src/lib/server/conversation/store.ts`: Updated `getLatestDecisionLog`, `getDecisionLog`, and `listDecisionLogs` to query `DecisionLogStore` with fallback to message metadata for 100% backward compatibility.
7. `src/app/api/sofia/decision-log/route.ts` & `src/app/api/sophia/decision-log/route.ts`: Updated GET endpoint for filtered queries and added POST endpoint for deterministic decision replay.
8. `src/app/api/sofia/why/route.ts` & `src/app/api/sophia/why/route.ts`: Updated GET endpoint to retrieve and format cited decision log entries.
9. `tests/sophia/g12_6_decision_log_and_explanation.test.ts`: Authored comprehensive test suite (18/18 tests pass).

## Why
Fulfills Gate G12.6 exit criteria:
- Authoritative decision log schema in `prisma/schema.prisma` and querying via `DecisionLogStore` and HTTP endpoints.
- 'Why did you do that' citations: Cites authoritative decision log entry ID, inputs, route, confidence, policy results, and outcomes without hallucinating state.
- Invariant 'no log entry means no action': Turn executor strictly enforces that if decision log persistence fails, downstream action is aborted and execution halts immediately.
