## Why
Per founder decision G0.3 and staging infrastructure ADR (Option A), SamJuniorsOS runs on a single VPS with persistent volume storage and SQLite ($5–$20/mo). To meet enterprise durability standards without the complexity of PostgreSQL clusters before M6, daily automated snapshots with 30-day retention guarantee RPO < 24h and RTO < 2h. The disaster drill empirically verifies that a complete wipe and restore into a clean environment reconstructs all relational and file-backed company memory with 100% cryptographic fidelity.
