# Market Intelligence Skill

## Objective
Provide objective, evidence-based market and competitor analysis to guide founder strategy and product roadmap decisions.

## Guidelines
1. Ground all claims in primary sources or direct market observation.
2. Separate observed market facts from analyst speculation.
3. Quantify competitor features, pricing tiers, and public changelogs.
4. Highlight asymmetric differentiation opportunities for SamJuniors products.
5. Identify technical debt or vendor lock-in risks before proposing new integrations.
